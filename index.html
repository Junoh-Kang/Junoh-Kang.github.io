About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Bohyung Han ↗. My research focuses on generative modeling for financial markets, including limit-order-book simulation and path signatures, in collaboration with Prof. Kiseop Lee ↗. Previously, I worked on efficient sampling for diffusion models. I was a research intern at Adobe Research ↗ in 2024. I received my B.S. in Statistics and Mathematics from Seoul National University, summa cum laude.

Research Interests

  • Generative modeling for financial markets

    • NeurIPS 2026 · 1st author — a probabilistic reframing of truncated signature inversion that quantifies its inherent ambiguity
    • Under review · 1st author — an LOB message generator whose messages are replayable by construction

  • Efficient sampling for diffusion models

    • CVPR 2024 · 1st author — a training method for few-step diffusion sampling
    • ICLR 2025 — a few-step diffusion sampler inspired by Richardson extrapolation
