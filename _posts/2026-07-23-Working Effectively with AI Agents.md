## Summary

- **Remember what productivity means.** It is not more output, but more verified outcomes with less human attention.
- **Stay attached to the Plan → Do → Verify loop.** Your mental model must evolve with the work; otherwise, you cannot plan or verify what comes next.
- **Remember the limits of being human.** Your bio-token budget is finite, cannot be parallelized, and is replenished only by time.
