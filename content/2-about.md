I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University, advised by [Prof. Bohyung Han](https://cv.snu.ac.kr/index.php/~bhhan/). I work on generative models for financial markets, currently limit-order-book simulation and path signatures, in collaboration with [Prof. Kiseop Lee](https://sites.google.com/view/purdue-dsfi/people). Previously, I worked on efficient sampling for diffusion models. My broader aim is principled probabilistic modeling of complex stochastic systems. Before my Ph.D., I received my B.S. in Statistics and Mathematics from Seoul National University, summa cum laude.
